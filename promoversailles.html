🏡 Versailles Alabang – Project Details

📍 Location

Versailles Alabang Village is located along Daang Hari Road near Las Piñas, Alabang and the Cavite border. It is across Ayala Alabang Village and accessible via MCX, SLEX and Skyway.

👉 Travel time: Makati/BGC approximately 20–30 minutes | NAIA approximately 20 minutes

🌟 Project Overview

A gated luxury subdivision with Mediterranean-inspired design, elevated surroundings and premium residential homes.

Clubhouse, swimming pools, gym, spa, tennis court, basketball court, parks, playgrounds and cabanas.

Near MCX exit with direct Daang Hari access and convenient connection to SLEX and Skyway.
